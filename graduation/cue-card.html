Graduation visit — presenter cues

Print & keep at desk · never on screen
Standing rule 1The offer is information, never pressure.
Standing rule 2Bad visit = slow down on slide 3. Never speed up to slide 7.
  1. CongratulationsReference one thing from her survey answers before advancing.
  2. Where you startedLet the quote sit — this slide is about remembering, not explaining.
  3. Then and now ⭐If the visit is going badly, slow down here — never speed up to the offer. Read the wins aloud in her words.
  4. Hormone pictureOne sentence per category, max. Don't teach endocrinology. (Skipped in then-test decks.)
  5. What made this workThe pivot slide — deliver the bridge line verbatim; it makes slide 6 a conclusion, not an upsell.
  6. What happens next"Most of our graduates continue with Ongoing Care — here's what both look like." Neutral voice; the layout steers.
  7. Your graduate offerVerbatim: "If you decide today or this week, it's $695. If you'd rather take time, it's $795 after — the program is identical either way." Then stop talking.
  8. First stepIf she said yes on 7, book the held slot now, in the room.
  9. Thank youWarm regardless of decision. Hand over the leave-behind with the deadline written in by hand.
